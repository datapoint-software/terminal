Datapoint Terminal's [license](LICENSE) lets you use, change and share the software. It doesn't give you rights to the Datapoint name or logo (see term 3 in [NOTICE](NOTICE)). This policy explains what you can do with them, so that people can always tell what Datapoint made and what it didn't.

## What this policy covers

- The names **"Datapoint"**, **"Datapoint Software"** and **"Datapoint Terminal"**, and the brand **"datapoint.software"**, used by Datapoint - Sistemas de Informação, Lda. (NIPC 514296631).
- The **Datapoint logo**, as provided in [`branding/datapoint-logo.svg`](branding/datapoint-logo.svg).

In the rest of this policy, "the Datapoint name" means any of these names.

## What you can do without asking

- **Keep the required attribution.** The application's legal notices, such as its About window, must show "Based on Datapoint Terminal by Datapoint - Sistemas de Informação, Lda." and the Datapoint logo (term 1 in [NOTICE](NOTICE)). Using the name and logo for that is always allowed.
- **Share unmodified copies** of Datapoint Terminal with the name and logo as they come.
- **Describe your work truthfully**, for example "based on Datapoint Terminal", "a fork of Datapoint Terminal", or "works with Datapoint Terminal".
- **Write about Datapoint Terminal**, in articles, talks, tutorials or reviews.

## What you can't do without permission

- Use the Datapoint name or logo as the name or logo of **your own product, fork or service**.
- Suggest that Datapoint **made, endorses or supports** your product, fork or service when it doesn't.
- Use the Datapoint name or logo in a **domain name, company name or social media account**, including names that could be confused with "datapoint.software".
- **Change** the Datapoint logo, or combine it with other logos, except to resize it.

## Modified versions

If you share a modified version, mark it as modified, as term 2 in [NOTICE](NOTICE) requires, and give it a name of its own, so users don't mistake it for Datapoint's. For example, "Acme Terminal, based on Datapoint Terminal" is fine. Calling it "Datapoint Terminal", "Terminal by Datapoint" or "Terminal by datapoint.software" isn't.

The required attribution stays in the application's legal notices either way: credit for the original work, not a claim that Datapoint made your version.
